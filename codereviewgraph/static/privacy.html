Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how CodeReviewGraph collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our website, authentication services, API, and multi-agent code auditing workstation.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to authenticate your account, secure your session, and run automated code reviews:

  • Google Account Information (via Google OAuth 2.0): When you sign in with Google, we request only standard identity scopes (openid, email, profile). We store your Google subject identifier (sub), verified email address, display name, profile picture URL, and login timestamps.
  • Email & One-Time Passcode (OTP) Authentication: When you sign in with email OTP, we collect your name and email address to generate a temporary 6-digit verification code and create your account record.
  • Session & Technical Metadata: To maintain secure sessions and defend against unauthorized access or CSRF attacks, we store a random session token along with basic request metadata (such as User-Agent and IP address) and single-use OAuth state tokens.
  • Submitted Source Code: When you execute an audit in the playground or via POST /api/audit, the source code snippet is analyzed in memory by the LangGraph workflow to generate findings and refactoring diffs.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Verify your identity and prevent duplicate accounts or unauthorized account linking.
  • Maintain an authenticated, HTTP-only browser session across the CodeReviewGraph workstation.
  • Display your profile name, email, and avatar inside the authenticated workspace.
  • Run deterministic AST parsing, security rule checks, and optional LLM-assisted code reviews upon your explicit request.
  • Protect our endpoints against abuse, brute-force OTP attempts, and replay attacks.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

CodeReviewGraph’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs strictly adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not access your Google Drive, Gmail, Calendar, or Contacts, we do not sell your Google user data, and we do not use Google user data for advertising or third-party AI model training.

4. Data Storage, Security & Retention

  • Cookies & Token Handling: Authentication sessions use an HTTP-only, SameSite=Lax cookie (crg_session) with Secure enforced over HTTPS. Sensitive credentials and tokens are never stored in browser localStorage.
  • Retention Periods: Email OTP codes expire automatically after 10 minutes (or immediately upon use or 5 failed verification attempts). OAuth CSRF state tokens expire after 10 minutes. Active login sessions expire after 7 days unless terminated earlier when you click Sign Out.
  • Code Audit Checkpoints: Workflow execution states are maintained in a bounded in-memory store (up to recent threads) and are not sold or shared with third parties. If Gemini LLM mode is enabled by your deployment operator, submitted snippets are sent to Google Gemini solely to produce your requested code review.

5. Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is only transmitted to infrastructure providers required to operate authentication and hosting (Google Identity Services / OAuth 2.0 for Google sign-in, your configured SMTP provider for OTP emails, and Vercel for application hosting).

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You may terminate your active session at any time by clicking Sign Out, which deletes your session record from our database and clears your session cookie. You may also revoke CodeReviewGraph’s access from your Google Account Permissions page. To request deletion of your stored profile record, contact the project maintainer using the details below.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data rights, please open an issue or reach out via our repository at github.com/LohithGopi/CodeReviewGraph.
